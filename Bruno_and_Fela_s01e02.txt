# BRUNO & FELa

## Episode 2 – The Great Race to the Bell

<break time="1s" />

It was another beautiful morning in the Forest of Whispering Leaves. Sunbeams danced between the branches, and the birds were holding a competition to see who could sing the loudest.

At least, that was what Fela believed.

[Fela] I'm sure that robin is winning!

Bruno looked up into the trees and replied that it wasn't a competition. The birds were simply singing.

[Fela] How do you know?

Bruno thought for a moment before explaining that he couldn't see any judges.

[Fela] [Laughter]

A little later, the two friends arrived at Forest School.

It had already been a week since their very first day, and Fela now knew most of the pupils. Most of the pupils knew Fela, too.

Mainly because it was rather difficult not to notice a squirrel who ran absolutely everywhere.

And she really did mean everywhere.

Even when there was no particular reason to run.

[Fela] Running is always necessary!

Bruno looked at her with curiosity as Fela explained that you never knew when you might suddenly need to run.

Bruno was just about to reply when something interesting happened in the schoolyard.

Leo the fox jumped onto a tree stump and called out for everyone's attention.

He announced that he had some very exciting news. Everyone immediately stopped what they were doing.

Even Frankie the little sparrow.

And that was quite unusual, because Frankie rarely stayed still for more than a few seconds.

[Fela] What's happening?

Leo grinned and announced that the Great Race to the Bell would take place after lessons that very day!

A wave of excited cheers swept across the schoolyard.

[Fela] Oh, yes! Finally, a competition made just for me!

Bruno looked at her suspiciously and asked whether she really thought the race was made especially for her.

[Fela] Of course! I'm the fastest!

Bruno raised an eyebrow and asked how she knew that.

[Fela] Because nobody has ever beaten me!

Bruno thought about this for a moment before asking whether she had ever raced against Leo.

[Fela] No.

Then he asked if she had ever raced against a deer.

[Fela] No.

Or a rabbit?

[Fela] Not that either.

Bruno nodded thoughtfully and concluded that they didn't really have enough information to be certain.

[Fela] Well, I have more than enough confidence!

[Fela] [Laughter]

During the lessons, Fela found it almost impossible to concentrate.

She could think about only one thing.

The race!

When Mrs. Owl was explaining how to recognise different animal tracks, Fela was busy drawing a trophy on her piece of paper.

When the class practised counting acorns, Fela was calculating how many steps ahead she would be.

And when lunchtime finally arrived, she had another very important question.

[Fela] Is it time for the race yet?

Mrs. Owl patiently explained that there was still some time before the lessons were over.

A few minutes later, Fela asked again.

And then again.

Mrs. Owl smiled and reminded her that the race would begin after school.

At last, the moment everyone had been waiting for arrived!

The contestants gathered at the starting line.

There was Leo the fox, Jasper the hedgehog, Clara the marten, and even Milo the little bear.

Although Milo admitted that he would much rather take part in a sleeping competition.

[Fela] [Laughter]

The racecourse was quite exciting. The runners had to go around the enormous oak tree, cross a little bridge over the stream, and then race all the way back to the schoolyard.

Leo stood at the starting line and asked everyone if they were ready.

[Fela] READY!

She shouted so loudly that a nearby bird flew out of a tree in surprise.

Leo laughed and announced that he didn't think anyone could possibly be more ready than that.

Bruno was sitting nearby, watching the contestants.

He quietly remarked that this was exactly what worried him.

[Fela] [Laughter]

Everyone took their positions.

There was a moment of silence.

Then came the starting signal!

And off they went!

Fela shot forward like a lightning bolt.

Within just a few seconds, she was in the lead.

[Fela] Ha! I told you!

She shouted triumphantly over her shoulder.

And that was when she made her first mistake.

Instead of looking where she was going...

She was looking behind her.

[Fela] Can everyone still see me?

And before she could even finish her question, she ran straight into a low fern bush.

WHUMP!

[Fela] Oh, dear!

The soft leaves and branches swallowed her up for a moment.

Meanwhile, the other contestants raced past.

First came Leo.

Then Jasper.

Then Clara.

And even Milo managed to get past her.

The little bear was running surprisingly fast for someone who had been dreaming about a sleeping competition just a few minutes earlier.

As he passed, he even managed to wave his paw at Fela.

[Fela] Hey! That's not fair!

Fela quickly scrambled out of the fern bush, shook a few leaves from her tail, and raced off again.

She ran like the wind.

She ran like a storm.

She ran so fast that she almost caught up with everyone.

Almost.

But not quite.

Leo crossed the finish line first.

Clara arrived just behind him.

Jasper came third.

And Fela crossed the finish line in fourth place.

She stopped and looked at the other contestants.

For a moment, she didn't say anything.

She felt disappointed.

Very disappointed.

[Fela] I should have won...

Bruno came over and sat beside her.

He told her that she had been incredibly fast.

[Fela] But I didn't win.

Bruno nodded and admitted that this was true.

[Fela] So you were right.

Bruno thought about that for a moment before explaining that he wasn't entirely right either.

Fela looked at him in surprise.

Bruno reminded her that she really had been fast.

The problem was that she had spent too much time checking whether everyone could see just how fast she was.

And not enough time watching where she was going.

[Fela] Oh...

For a little while, they sat quietly together.

Then Fela admitted that it sounded a little silly.

Bruno asked whether she meant just a little silly.

[Fela] [Laughter]

A moment later, even Fela started laughing.

And soon, the disappointment was almost completely gone.

Just then, Leo came over carrying something special.

It was a medal made from a beautiful green leaf, with a little piece of string attached to it.

He proudly presented it to Fela and announced that it was a very special award.

The prize was for the most spectacular dive into a fern bush in the history of Forest School!

[Fela] [Laughter]

Everyone laughed, including Fela.

She put on her leafy medal and decided that perhaps finishing fourth wasn't such a terrible thing after all.

Especially when you received a medal for something nobody else had ever managed to do!

That evening, as Bruno and Fela made their way home, Fela thought about everything that had happened.

Not just about losing the race.

Not even about the fern bush.

She thought about something much more important.

Sometimes, believing in yourself is a wonderful thing.

It gives you courage to try new things, face challenges, and keep going even when something seems difficult.

But it's just as important to remember that other people can surprise you, too.

Someone who seems slow might turn out to be surprisingly fast.

Someone who prefers sleeping competitions might suddenly become a brilliant runner.

And someone who is absolutely certain they will win might discover that there is still a lot to learn.

That day, Fela learned that confidence is a wonderful thing.

As long as it doesn't stop you from noticing what's happening right in front of your nose.

Especially when what's right in front of your nose happens to be a very large fern bush!
