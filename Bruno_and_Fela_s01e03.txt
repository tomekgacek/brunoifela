# BRUNO & FELА

## Episode 3 – The Mysterious Hedgehog House

<break time="1s" />

Autumn was slowly making its way through the Whispering Leaves Forest. The trees were beginning to change their colors, painting the forest in beautiful shades of gold, orange, and red. Some leaves were bright yellow, others were deep red, and a few seemed unable to decide which color they liked best. They simply changed their minds every time the wind blew.

A gentle breeze carried the smell of damp earth and fallen leaves through the forest. Squirrels hurried around collecting acorns, birds prepared their nests for the colder days ahead, and everywhere you looked, the forest was busy getting ready for autumn.

Meanwhile, classes were underway at Forest School.

Mrs. Owl had prepared something special for her students that morning. She stood at the front of the classroom, wearing her usual little spectacles and smiling mysteriously.

"Today, my dear students, you are going to build small shelters for the forest animals!" she announced.

The classroom immediately burst into excitement. Some students jumped up and down, while others started whispering about their brilliant ideas. Frankie the little sparrow was already flapping his wings excitedly, and Leo the fox was trying to imagine what his shelter might look like.

And Bruno...

Bruno took out a sheet of paper.

Then another sheet of paper.

Then a pencil.

And finally, just in case, a spare pencil.

Fela watched him with a familiar expression.

[Fela] "Oh, no. Here we go again. He's going to draw plans!"

Bruno looked at her calmly, as if this were the most obvious thing in the world.

"Well, that's exactly what plans are for," he explained. "Before you build something, you need to know what you're building."

[Fela] "And that's exactly what paws are for! You can just start building!"

Bruno adjusted his glasses, even though he wasn't wearing any.

"That's not how building works, Fela."

[Fela] "Maybe not for you!"

Mrs. Owl clapped her wings to get everyone's attention.

"Now, I will divide you into pairs. Each team will have a different animal to build a shelter for. Remember, your shelters must be safe, comfortable, and strong enough to protect their little residents from the autumn rain."

She looked at her list.

"Bruno and Fela, you will work together. Your task is to build a house for Jasper the hedgehog."

Fela immediately jumped with excitement.

[Fela] "A house for Jasper? Fantastic! Let's go! We need branches! And leaves! And maybe some moss! Lots of moss!"

Before Bruno could even open his notebook, Fela was already racing towards the forest.

Bruno sighed, looked at his carefully prepared paper, and followed her.

A few minutes later, Fela returned carrying an enormous pile of branches. It was so big that she was almost completely hidden behind it. Only her little tail could be seen sticking out from one side.

[Fela] "I've got everything!"

She dropped the branches onto the ground with a loud crash.

"Everything?" Bruno asked, looking at the enormous pile.

[Fela] "Absolutely everything! Well... almost everything. I haven't found any chocolate yet, but I don't think hedgehogs need chocolate in their houses."

Bruno carefully examined the pile.

"This looks more like a small forest than a house."

[Fela] "That's because it's going to be a very special house!"

Bruno unfolded his drawing and proudly placed it on the ground. It showed a detailed design of a small wooden shelter. There were walls, a roof, a little entrance, and even a tiny window.

But that wasn't all.

In one corner, Bruno had written:

"Special shelf for extremely important pinecones."

Fela stared at the drawing.

[Fela] "Wait a minute. Do hedgehogs have shelves for pinecones?"

Bruno shrugged.

"I haven't asked Jasper yet. But everyone needs somewhere to keep important things."

[Fela] "And what makes a pinecone extremely important?"

"That's a very good question," Bruno replied. "We'll have to investigate."

Fela shook her head, trying not to laugh.

They finally agreed to start building.

At least, they thought they had agreed.

For the next hour, they tried to work together. Bruno carefully measured the branches and arranged them according to his drawing. Fela, meanwhile, kept bringing more and more materials.

Bruno placed a branch on the left.

Fela added three more on the right.

Bruno adjusted the wall.

Fela moved it somewhere else.

Bruno started measuring the roof.

Fela had already put it on.

Bruno tried to make the entrance a little wider.

Fela decided it needed to be much bigger.

And so it went on.

Every time Bruno tried to follow his plan, Fela changed something. And every time Fela added a new idea, Bruno wanted to check whether it would actually work.

Eventually, the little shelter began to look rather strange. One wall was taller than the other, the roof was leaning to one side, and the entrance was so narrow that even a very small hedgehog might have trouble getting inside.

Finally, both friends sat down, exhausted.

[Fela] "This isn't working at all!"

Bruno looked at the half-finished shelter.

"That's because you're not following the plan."

[Fela] "And maybe it's because you spend more time looking at your plan than actually building anything!"

Bruno frowned.

"Well, things don't work properly when you build them without thinking first!"

[Fela] "And they don't get built at all when you spend the whole day thinking!"

For a moment, they stared at each other.

Then they turned away.

They were both very annoyed.

So annoyed, in fact, that they didn't even look at each other.

And that was quite difficult, considering they were sitting right next to each other.

<break time="1s" />

Just then, Milo the little bear happened to walk past. He was carrying a basket full of mushrooms he had collected during the morning.

He stopped when he saw the strange-looking shelter.

Then he looked at Bruno.

Then at Fela.

Then back at the shelter.

"What happened here?" he asked.

"Nothing," Bruno replied.

"Everything," Fela added at exactly the same time.

Milo scratched his head.

He looked at their unfinished construction for a moment and then said just one thing.

"Maybe instead of sitting around doing nothing, you could try working together?"

And with that, he picked up his basket and continued walking.

Fela watched him disappear between the trees.

[Fela] "Was that all his advice?"

Bruno looked thoughtful.

"Apparently, yes."

They sat quietly for a few more seconds.

Then Bruno glanced at Fela.

Fela glanced at Bruno.

And suddenly, they both started laughing.

Because sometimes the simplest advice is the best advice.

[Fela] "All right. How about this? You make the plan, and I'll do the building."

Bruno thought about it.

"Well, that's almost a good idea."

[Fela] "Almost?"

"Perhaps we should do both together. We can plan and build at the same time."

[Fela] "Hmm. That sounds suspiciously sensible."

Bruno smiled.

"Thank you. I try my best."

[Fela] "Don't get too excited. We still have a house to build!"

This time, they decided to listen to each other.

Bruno examined the materials and explained how the walls should be arranged. Fela searched for the strongest branches and brought them over one by one.

Bruno checked whether the structure was stable.

Fela found some soft moss to make the floor comfortable.

Bruno worked out how to make the roof waterproof.

Fela quickly collected large leaves to cover it.

Whenever Bruno had an idea, he explained it to Fela. And whenever Fela thought of a better way to do something, Bruno listened carefully.

Sometimes they agreed immediately.

Sometimes they had to try two or three different solutions.

But this time, neither of them got annoyed.

And something wonderful began to happen.

The little house started to take shape.

Its walls were strong and sturdy. The entrance was wide enough for Jasper to walk through comfortably, and the roof was carefully covered with leaves and moss to keep the rain out.

Fela even found a soft patch of moss for the floor, making the inside feel warm and cosy.

Bruno stepped back and examined their work.

"It looks very good," he said proudly.

[Fela] "Very good? It looks fantastic!"

Bruno smiled.

"You're right. It looks fantastic."

Fela looked around the little house.

[Fela] "So... what about the special shelf for extremely important pinecones?"

Bruno thought for a moment.

"Perhaps we can add it another day."

[Fela] "I knew you wouldn't forget!"

They both burst out laughing.

And just in time, too, because Mrs. Owl had announced that it was time for everyone to present their shelters.

One by one, the students showed their creations.

Leo had built a small shelter for a family of mice. Clara the pine marten had made a beautiful little hideaway using branches and colorful leaves. Frankie had even managed to create a tiny shelter for some of the smaller birds.

Finally, it was Bruno and Fela's turn.

Mrs. Owl looked at their creation with interest.

"Well, well! This looks very promising. And who is going to test it?"

At that very moment, Jasper the hedgehog slowly approached.

He looked at the little house.

Then he looked at Bruno and Fela.

"Is this really for me?" he asked.

Bruno nodded.

"Yes, Jasper. We built it especially for you."

Fela couldn't hide her excitement.

[Fela] "Go on! Take a look inside!"

Jasper carefully walked towards the entrance. He sniffed the moss, looked at the walls, and slowly stepped inside.

Everyone waited.

The classroom became completely silent.

A few seconds passed.

Then they heard a little voice coming from inside.

"Ooooh! This is really cosy!"

Jasper poked his little nose out of the entrance.

"I love it! It's warm, it's soft, and the roof looks strong enough to keep the rain out. And I can fit inside without bumping my nose!"

The students began clapping.

Fela jumped into the air.

[Fela] "We did it! We really did it!"

Bruno smiled proudly.

"Yes, we did. And I must admit, it turned out even better than my original plan."

Mrs. Owl smiled warmly.

"Well done, Bruno and Fela. You have built a wonderful home for Jasper. But I think you've also learned something even more important today."

Bruno and Fela looked at each other.

"What is that?" Bruno asked.

Mrs. Owl adjusted her spectacles.

"That working together doesn't mean doing everything exactly the same way. It means listening to one another, sharing ideas, and finding a way to combine your strengths."

Fela nodded thoughtfully.

Bruno nodded too.

And Jasper, who was still sitting comfortably inside his new house, added:

"And it means remembering to make the entrance big enough for a hedgehog!"

Everyone laughed.

<break time="1s" />

On their way home, Bruno and Fela walked slowly along the forest path. The afternoon sun was shining through the colorful leaves, and the wind gently rustled the branches above them.

For a while, neither of them said anything.

Then Fela finally spoke.

[Fela] "You know, I think I wanted to do everything my own way today."

Bruno nodded.

"I think I did too."

He looked down at the path and kicked a little leaf with his foot.

"Sometimes I feel that everyone should make a plan before doing anything. And sometimes I forget that a good idea can come from someone who doesn't have a plan at all."

Fela smiled.

[Fela] "And sometimes I think that the best thing to do is just start building. But I forget that it's easier when you know what you're building!"

Bruno chuckled.

"Well, I suppose we're both a little bit right."

[Fela] "And a little bit wrong!"

They both burst out laughing.

As they continued walking, Bruno suddenly stopped.

"Actually, Fela, I have another idea."

[Fela] "Oh, no. Not another plan!"

"Don't worry. It's a very small one."

[Fela] "How small?"

"Just a little shelf for extremely important pinecones."

Fela shook her head and laughed.

[Fela] "I knew it! You really haven't forgotten about that shelf!"

"Of course not. Some things are simply too important to forget."

And so, that day, Bruno and Fela learned something they would remember for a long time.

Not everyone has to think in the same way.

Not everyone has to do things in exactly the same way.

Sometimes one friend has a brilliant plan, while another has the energy and courage to get things started.

And when we listen to each other, share our ideas, and combine what we're good at, we can create something much better than either of us could have made alone.

The little house they built for Jasper stood proudly among the trees for many months.

It kept him warm and dry through the autumn rain and the cold winter nights.

And, as the story goes, someone eventually added a small shelf inside for extremely important pinecones.

Bruno, of course, insisted that he had absolutely nothing to do with it.

But nobody believed him.

**End of Episode 3 – The Mysterious Hedgehog House**
