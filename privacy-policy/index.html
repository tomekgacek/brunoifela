Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

App designed for children (Made for Kids)

1. Data Controller

The controller of the "Bruno i Fela" mobile app (the "App") is Tomasz Gacek, contact e-mail: gacek.tomek@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data of its users to any servers. It does not require creating an account, logging in, or providing any identifying information (name, e-mail address, location, etc.).

The App contains no advertising and does not use any analytics or tracking tools (e.g. Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics), and does not share any data with third parties.

3. Data stored locally on the device

The App may store on the user's device only local settings and preferences (e.g. playback state, last selected episode) to provide a better user experience. This data never leaves the device and is never transmitted anywhere.

4. Photo library access

The App may request access to the photo library solely to save coloring pages or images selected by the user to the device. This access is used only at the moment of saving a file and does not involve reading or transmitting any other photos from the device.

5. External content and links

The App contains links to content published on YouTube and Spotify. Tapping such a link opens the browser or the YouTube/Spotify app, which operate under their own, independent privacy policies:

  • YouTube / Google Privacy Policy
  • Spotify Privacy Policy

We encourage parents and guardians to review these policies before children open external links.

6. Purchases and payments

The App contains no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or advertising.

7. Children's privacy

The App is designed for children and is marked as "Made for Kids". In line with regulations such as COPPA and GDPR, the App does not collect any personal data from children or from any other users.

8. Security

Because the App does not transmit or store personal data on servers, the risk related to personal data security is kept to a minimum.

9. Changes to this privacy policy

We reserve the right to update this privacy policy. Any changes will be published on this page along with the date of the last update.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at: gacek.tomek@gmail.com.
