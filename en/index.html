Meet the characters

Bruno and Fela have very different personalities. That is what makes their adventures so much fun and helps them learn from each other.

Bruno the beaver

Bruno is a clever little beaver who likes to think things through. He loves making plans, finding smart solutions and keeping his world in order. He often carries a notebook to jot down ideas and observations. Sometimes he overthinks things, but that helps him notice what others might miss.

Fela the squirrel

Fela is quick, full of energy and always ready for action. She bravely heads wherever something interesting is happening, and new adventures are impossible to resist. She can be spontaneous and act before she has a chance to think things through. Her enthusiasm, energy and big heart bring joy and a little daring to every story.
